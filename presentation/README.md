## Deck summary

The 7-page presentation introduces LUNA-SCOPE and Team VayuTej, frames the four interacting site-selection considerations (terrain, solar access, Earth communication and mission feasibility), describes the product workflow, presents terrain-analysis capabilities, explains solar and Earth-visibility considerations, summarizes mission intelligence and the deterministic Copilot concept, and closes with impact, references and future directions.
